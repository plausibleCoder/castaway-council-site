Castaway Council

A pixel island colony run by its viewers. A new 20-second episode is posted every day on Instagram and YouTube Shorts, and the comments decide what the castaways do next.

  1. Watch the day's episode.
  2. Write what the colony should do, in your own words, in any language or with emoji.
  3. The next episode shows what the crowd chose, and what happened.
