Privacy policy

Castaway Council · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Who we are

Castaway Council is an interactive story show posted on Instagram (@castawaycouncil) and YouTube (@castawaycouncil). It is run by an individual in Jacksonville, Florida, USA. Contact: castawaycouncil@gmail.com.

What we collect

An automated program reads the public comments on our own Instagram and YouTube posts, through the official Instagram API and YouTube API Services. For each comment it reads the commenter's public username, the comment text, its time, and its like count. We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, private messages, or anything from accounts other than comments on our own posts.

How we use it

  • To count each commenter's idea as one vote in the next episode.
  • On Instagram, a commenter's public username, and sometimes a short quote of their comment, may appear in the next episode's video and caption as credit. Comments about politics, religion, real people, or that seem to come from children under 13 are never quoted.
  • The show's Instagram account may reply to comments automatically. These replies are written by an AI model that reads the comment, and always end with 🤖. On YouTube, there are no AI replies; the show may post one short reply under the day's winning comment and a daily results comment.
  • Each episode may have a narrator voice made with Google Gemini text-to-speech. The day's winner's username can be spoken in it; no other names are.
  • The YouTube videos follow their own story, decided only by YouTube comments. Like on Instagram, the day's winner is named in that video, its spoken narration and its description: their public YouTube channel name and a short summary of their idea. No other YouTube usernames or comment text are shown.
  • To make simple statistics about the show (for example, how many people took part). These are counts, not profiles of people.

We do not sell or share this data with advertisers, and we do not use it to profile anyone.

Who processes it

To run the show we use: GitHub (runs the program and stores an encrypted save file), Supabase (temporarily stores each episode video so Instagram can download it), and an AI service that reads comment text to understand ideas in any language and to filter abuse: currently Google Gemini (Google AI Studio), and possibly Anthropic Claude in the future. Comment text and usernames are sent to that service only for this purpose. Google may use data sent to its free Gemini tier to improve its products, as described in the Gemini API terms. These comments are already public. These services process the data only to run the show.

How long we keep it

Comment text and usernames are deleted after 30 days. People who voted are stored only as a one-way code (not their username) and are also deleted after 30 days. Stored episode videos are deleted after 30 days. Videos already posted on Instagram and YouTube stay there until we remove them.

Your choices

  • To have your name left out of future episodes, or your stored data deleted, email castawaycouncil@gmail.com with your username. We act on requests within 7 days.
  • You can also simply delete your comment on Instagram or YouTube.
  • The YouTube channel owner can revoke this program's access to YouTube at any time at Google security settings.

YouTube

This show uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our YouTube channel you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The show is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If someone says they are under 13, they are not counted, and their stored data is deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
